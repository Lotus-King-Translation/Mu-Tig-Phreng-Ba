# Translation endnote policy

Translate the fixed `golden-v1` reading. Endnotes must distinguish the physical Adzom1973–77 print from the Valby/Wikisource electronic transcript used to prepare it.

1. **Adzom difference:** give exact golden and printed wording where legible, affected pair/golden anchors, printed page/image and allocated native evidence, what differs, and the effect on the English. Include material absent from either side. An uncertain source reading is never certified agreement.
2. **Transcript correction:** reproduce the exact old/new wording and explain that the golden correction agrees with Adzom; it is not a departure from the print. Cover all 30 changed anchors, even when a difference has no English consequence.
3. **Source omission/layer:** identify every locally observed omitted smaller note or source heading/graphic, with its location and readable wording or explicit untranscribed status. Do not insert it silently into the translated root text. Preserve all 96 existing golden uncertainty records and any additional audit findings.
4. **Display/metadata:** electronic chapter/page controls, line division, absent print shads and decorative punctuation are documented as presentation conventions. Audit records identify the affected page/span; these do not become invented lexical variants. Reader notes distinguish main text, annotations and colophons.
5. **Translation issue:** record syntax, interpretive supply, terminology gaps and provisional contextual usages separately. Keep exact Tibetan, location, issue, working treatment, uncertainty and review action. None of these alters the golden text or canonical glossary.

One note may cover a coherent span, but every affected source object must link to it. Notes use stable IDs and occur at the end of the English publication, with markers at their corresponding translation pairs. No generic note may conceal an unexamined span or stand in for an exact readable difference.

The new comparison covers every governing page's main-text span in the active chapter. It does not assert complete decipherment or translation of all printed commentary. Unreadable or untranscribed source notes receive local omission records. Discovery of a difference does not authorize rewriting the fixed golden release; explain it in the translation apparatus.
