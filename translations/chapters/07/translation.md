Then the holder of the vajra
petitioned Vajradhara in these words:[^T07-001]

<!-- pair: MTP-000455 -->

“Are sentient beings and the phenomena of saṃsāra and nirvāṇa
one, or are they different?
I have doubts about that.
Bhagavān, speak to me.”[^T07-001]

<!-- pair: MTP-000456 -->

Then that great Vajradhara
rose from the seat of bliss
and solemnly uttered these words:[^T07-001]

<!-- pair: MTP-000457 -->

“The phenomena of saṃsāra are like this:
since this is absent [at] the very beginning,
just as imputed through delusion about objects,
[they] appear within the domain of sentient beings.[^T07-001][^T07-002]

<!-- pair: MTP-000458 -->

These, moreover, are as follows:
earth, water, fire and wind;[^T07-003]

<!-- pair: MTP-000459 -->

[Printed page 503.]

<!-- pair: MTP-000460 -->

space, sun and moon;
the [གཟུངས: unresolved] of males and females;
planets, stars and clouds;
trees, mountains and rocks;[^T07-003]

<!-- pair: MTP-000461 -->

roots, trunks and branches;
leaves, flowers and fruit;[^T07-004]

<!-- pair: MTP-000462 -->

smell, taste, sound and touch;
music, pennants and parasols;
victory banners, canopies and so forth—[^T07-005]

<!-- pair: MTP-000463 -->

these phenomena, appearing as they revolve,
are complete [in] the yogin’s own body.[^T07-006]

<!-- pair: MTP-000464 -->

Earth is flesh, entity itself.
Water is blood, gathering itself.
Fire is heat, ripening the entire body.
Wind is breath, holding the body’s vitality.
Space is ordinary mind, holding the body’s material presence.[^T07-007]

<!-- pair: MTP-000465 -->

Sun and moon are eyes, making clear.
Males are birth itself;
females appear as sustaining life.[^T07-007]

<!-- pair: MTP-000466 -->

Planets are the mouth, and stars are teeth.
Clouds are hair, and trees are channels.[^T07-007]

<!-- pair: MTP-000467 -->

[Printed page 504.]

<!-- pair: MTP-000468 -->

Mountains are the body, and rocks are bones.
Roots are the heart, and the trunk is the legs.
Branches are the limbs;
flowers themselves are the five faculties.
Fruit is established as the body.[^T07-004][^T07-008]

<!-- pair: MTP-000469 -->

Smell is body hair, and taste is skin.
Sound is the voice, and touch is illness.[^T07-005][^T07-009]

<!-- pair: MTP-000470 -->

Music is the internal organs, and pennants are water.
Parasols are feet, and victory banners are names.
Canopies are vapor.
These are complete in the yogin’s body.[^T07-005][^T07-006][^T07-008]

<!-- pair: MTP-000471 -->

Awareness itself is as follows:
the five elements are the five lights;
sun and moon are discerning knowing and means.
The male is the Ground; [བུད་མེད་པས: female relation unresolved].
Planets are the measure of realization, and stars are phenomena.[^T07-010]

<!-- pair: MTP-000472 -->

[Printed page 505.]

<!-- pair: MTP-000473 -->

Clouds are compassionate responsiveness, pervading all.
Trees are the path of the nature of phenomena’s expansion.
Mountains are the view, without change.
Rocks are dharma embodiment, free from destruction.[^T07-011]

<!-- pair: MTP-000474 -->

Roots are dharma embodiment, the single cause.
Branches are the limbs of awakening itself.
The trunk is one taste in emptiness.
Leaves are the pervading basic space of phenomena.
Flowers are the lamp of primordial knowing itself.
The fruit is the three embodiments, complete in themselves.[^T07-004][^T07-011]

<!-- pair: MTP-000475 -->

Sound is the sound of the domain of the nature of phenomena.
Smell resounds as the buddhas’ speech.
Taste is experiential acquaintance, the taste of bliss.
Touch is the garment of great meditative stability.[^T07-005][^T07-012]

<!-- pair: MTP-000476 -->

Music is the complete maṇḍala of the five lights.
Pennants are colors, limpid through clarity.
Parasols are protection, the radiance of discerning knowing.
Canopies are the key point of the greatness of pith instructions.
Victory banners are realization, manifestly buddha.[^T07-005][^T07-013]

<!-- pair: MTP-000477 -->

[Printed page 506.]

<!-- pair: MTP-000478 -->

The phenomena of the essence of ordinary mind itself
abide primordially in the manner of spontaneous presence.
Therefore, the phenomena of [བླ] and [དངོས་བོ]
are [བརྒགས་པ: unresolved] itself.[^T07-014]

<!-- pair: MTP-000479 -->

O holder of the vajra, listen well!
The white phenomena of transcendence
are all complete in body and ordinary mind.
For the phenomena of saṃsāra are [འདས་སྟོང: relation unresolved]:[^T07-001][^T07-006][^T07-015]

<!-- pair: MTP-000480 -->

maṇḍalas, deities and offerings;
mantras, mudrās and deep absorption;
empowerment and commitments;
bestowal of pith instructions and experiential acquaintance;
likewise the generation stage;
hearing, reflection and cultivation;[^T07-016]

<!-- pair: MTP-000481 -->

[Printed page 507.]

<!-- pair: MTP-000482 -->

view, activity and cultivation;
generosity, ethical discipline and patience;
diligence, meditative stability and discerning knowing;
strength, aspiration and primordial knowing;
likewise the perfection of means;
activity in the ten virtues, and so forth.[^T07-017]

<!-- pair: MTP-000483 -->

Within means and discerning knowing themselves, moreover:
basic space, awareness and appearance;
embodiments and primordial knowing, and their mode of abiding;
the Ground, the path and the result;
realization and the manner of realization;
the five lights, colors and objects themselves;[^T07-018]

<!-- pair: MTP-000484 -->

[Printed page 508.]

<!-- pair: MTP-000485 -->

emptiness and the absence of conceptual elaborations;
essence, intrinsic nature and compassionate responsiveness;
[ངང་དངས: unresolved], intrinsic nature and great identity;
primordial purity itself and spontaneous presence;
the Ground of the common domain and the Ground of delusion.[^T07-019]

<!-- pair: MTP-000486 -->

Emaho!
All this appears as the domain of what is to be known:[^T07-020]

<!-- pair: MTP-000487 -->

buddha, Dharma and Saṅgha;
the teaching, place, retinue and time;
dharma embodiment, complete enjoyment embodiment and emanation embodiment;
the sūtra collection, Vinaya and great Abhidharma;
hearers, solitary victors and [བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས: identification unresolved];[^T07-021]

<!-- pair: MTP-000488 -->

the hearers’ Great Assembly school,
the Proponents of All-Existence, the Honored-by-All,
and [གནས་བསྟན: school identification unresolved]—the four root schools;[^T07-022]

<!-- pair: MTP-000489 -->

suffering, origin and cessation,
the truth of the path, and so forth, distinguished;
those with outflows and those without outflows;
those beyond the stages and those abiding [in them].”[^T07-023]
