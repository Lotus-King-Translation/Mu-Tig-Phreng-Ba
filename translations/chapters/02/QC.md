## Findings and dispositions

All proposed corrections were checked against the same terminology and source-preservation requirements. The translator or coordinator made the changes; this reviewer did not rewrite the English or Tibetan.

1. **CH02-Q01 — confirmed terminology/documentation error; Medium severity, High confidence; corrected.** At S180, S181 and S207, exact source `སྐྱེ་མེད` initially became “unborn,” described in T02-018 as a grammatical realization of canonical `སྐྱེ་བ་མེད་པ་` → **non-arising**. The synonym was not ordinary inflection of that English term. All three occurrences now retain “non-arising,” and T02-018 explains the shortened source-form linkage. This correction preserves intelligible English and does not amend the glossary. Rules: Part I §§3–4; Q1, Q5–Q6.

2. **CH02-Q02 — probable meaning/scope error; Medium severity, Moderate confidence; corrected with a retained review flag.** S135 `དུག་ལྔ་མདོར་ཕྱུང་སྨན་གྱི་ངང` initially read “extract the five poisons at their source—the state of medicine.” The summary/essential-point construction `མདོར་ཕྱུང` does not securely establish that source relation. The author changed this to “sum up the five poisons—the state of medicine.” T02-011 and the proposal preserve uncertainty rather than claiming a settled technical interpretation. The correction concerns this occurrence, not a universal equivalent for `ཕྱུང`. Rules: Part I §§4–7; Q3, Q6, Q9.

3. **CH02-Q03 — probable syntax/scope error; Medium severity, Moderate confidence; corrected.** S205 `དངོས་པོ་དྲན་བྱེད་ཡིད་ལས་འདས` initially read “it is beyond entities and the mental faculty that recalls.” The main wording now reads “it is beyond the mental faculty that recalls entities.” Taking entities as the recalled object more directly respects the modifier sequence and avoids adding coordination that independently broadens the scope of transcendence. T02-022 keeps the wider coordination as an alternative for human review. **Mental faculty** remains unchanged. Rules: Part I §§4–5; Q3, Q9.

4. **CH02-Q04 — source-syntax open question, Medium potential severity, High confidence in the visible genitive; represented as unresolved.** S141 `དོན་དམ་ཀུན་རྫོབ་གཉིས་མེད་པའི` ends with a genitive whose head is not secure. The author's revision from supplied “[where]” to “of the nonduality of the ultimate and the conventional—[attachment unresolved]” was checked and accepted as a transparent unresolved treatment. Pair 43 is marked unresolved; T02-011 identifies possible preceding/following attachments without settling one. This records the author's correction, not a claim that QC independently resolved the sentence. Rules: Q2–Q3, Q7.

5. **CH02-Q05 — confirmed presentation problem; Low severity, High confidence; corrected.** Pair 55's quoted `རིག་པའི་སྣང་བ་འོད་གསལ་བ` now uses single quotation marks inside the continuing outer speech. No quotation wording or abbreviation scope was supplied. Rules: Q8–Q9.

6. **CH02-Q06 — apparatus documentation gaps; Medium severity, High confidence; corrected.** Native notes C02-008 and C02-103 contained readable Tibetan fragments but did not give their English sense. The former now provisionally explains `དྲུང་དུ` as “in the presence of” or “before”; the latter applies established **vajra chains** to `ལུ་གུ་རྒྱུད`. Both remain apparatus-only, with attachment uncertainty explicit. Other untranscribed words are not thereby claimed translated. The audit and reader notes were updated together and resealed.
