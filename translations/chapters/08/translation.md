petitioned Vajradhara in these words:[^T08-001]

and intrinsic nature of the secret continuum?

[is] what is indicated and what indicates.[^T08-002]

<!-- pair: MTP-000576 -->

Pervasion has two aspects:
pervasion by emptiness and pervasion by clarity.[^T08-002]

<!-- pair: MTP-000577 -->

Emptiness is one cause; because [it] lacks material presence,
[it] transcends the phenomena of all entities.
[དངོས་ཏེ: construction unresolved], without appearance, with a single pervasion,
[it] becomes the Ground of all, one essence—

<!-- pair: MTP-000581 -->

Pervasively spread, without material presence, empty in essence,
stainless, unmixed and complete—
free from conceptualization, the intrinsic nature of emptiness;
because [it] pervades through emptiness, [it] is called “continuum.”[^T08-006]

<!-- pair: MTP-000582 -->

Clear, since intrinsic nature is unceasing,
illuminating, appearing and pervading all,
clear and warm and cool,
[it] holds the identity of lightness and motion.[^T08-007]

<!-- pair: MTP-000583 -->

Pervasion, primordial radiance and self-clarity;
that which liberates, the very seed of ripening—

<!-- pair: MTP-000584 -->

The simultaneous type and self-liberation,
self-arising; through clarity, [it] is connected with the body,
stainless and separately limpid.[^T08-009]

<!-- pair: MTP-000585 -->

In object-appearance, clear and free from grasping,
the knowing of the five gates is self-clear and complete.
Because clarity pervades the body, [it is] continuum.[^T08-010]

self-liberation without exertion and effort,

As for the gate: that itself is undeluded,

<!-- pair: MTP-000593 -->

The key points of the object also have three aspects:
the pure basic space stays,
likewise the essence of awareness stays,

<!-- pair: MTP-000594 -->

Ordinary mind, without movement, has mindfulness cleared.
Awareness does not move; the three embodiments are clear.[^T08-016]

<!-- pair: MTP-000595 -->

With the mental faculty and ordinary mind at ease, the flow of mindfulness ceases.
With body and speech at ease, [there is] freedom from suffering.
With awareness at ease, [it] arises as five embodiments.[^T08-017]

Through clear light arising, the subtle and coarse cease.
Through basic space and awareness arising, objects and ordinary mind cease.[^T08-018]

complete in one’s own continuum without being generated—
what indicates is the means;
abiding in continuity is called “continuum.”[^T08-019]

endowed with the indivisible intrinsic nature of vajra,
of the single Ground of its own indication,
[its] intrinsic nature is one in emptiness itself;
[it is] the domain that generates complete buddhahood.[^T08-021]
