<!-- pair: MTP-000187 -->

Then the wrathful ones of the families petitioned in these words:[^T05-001]

<!-- pair: MTP-000188 -->

“O, O Bhagavān Vajradhara!
What is the intrinsic nature of all buddhas?
How does it abide in sentient beings?[^T05-001][^T05-002]

<!-- pair: MTP-000189 -->

If that itself abides, delusion would be unfitting.
As for the manner in which that delusion is reversed,
by what and by what is it reversed?
What is undeluded primordial knowing like?[^T05-002]

<!-- pair: MTP-000190 -->

If we have become [—],
how is there twofold appearance in that?
Lord, how is doubt dispelled?”[^T05-002]

<!-- pair: MTP-000191 -->

[Printed page 455.]

<!-- pair: MTP-000192 -->

Vajradhara spoke:[^T05-001]

<!-- pair: MTP-000193 -->

“Supreme Lord of Secrets, holder of the vajra!
The two—the Ground, one in essence; from distinction—
abide in the unique enlightened intent without conceptual elaborations,
endowed with the intrinsic nature of the sphere of great bliss:
one mandala, lord of all buddhas.[^T05-001][^T05-003]

<!-- pair: MTP-000194 -->

Through the primordial purity of essence itself,
there is spontaneous presence in intrinsic nature itself.[^T05-004]

<!-- pair: MTP-000195 -->

Beyond grounds, directions, and divisions,
there is no existence, nonexistence, or seeming appearance;
there is no one, two, or object of enumeration.
In great emptiness, [it] is established just as it settles.[^T05-004]

<!-- pair: MTP-000196 -->

[Printed page 456.]

<!-- pair: MTP-000197 -->

It is not the domain of scriptural transmission and reasoning;
tantras and pith instructions do not indicate it.
Through view, cultivation, and activity,
its meanings do not become known.[^T05-005]

<!-- pair: MTP-000198 -->

As for its meaning, there is no accomplishment of a result;
through phenomena, that itself becomes obscured.
It is not accomplished through thinking,
nor can it be examined through discerning knowing.[^T05-005]

<!-- pair: MTP-000199 -->

Through generosity, ethical discipline, and patience,
that itself is free from the results of harm and benefit.
Through hearing, thinking, and cultivation,
even if [it] is examined supremely, the meaning is not seen.[^T05-006]

<!-- pair: MTP-000200 -->

There is no mandala in that;
there is no deity, no seed of generation.
What would one do with mantra and seals?
There is no need to elaborate offerings and the like.
Where are empowerment and commitments?[^T05-007]

<!-- pair: MTP-000201 -->

[Printed page 457.]

<!-- pair: MTP-000202 -->

There is no approach or accomplishment;
there is no elaboration of wheels.
There is no protection and no object of protection;
[it] is beyond the domain of obstructing spirits and harm.[^T05-007]

<!-- pair: MTP-000203 -->

It is that, and is also beings;
it is ordinary mind and what arises from ordinary mind.
It is many, and it is unique;
I myself am saṃsāra and nirvāṇa.[^T05-008]

<!-- pair: MTP-000204 -->

It is gradual entry into mandalas and the like;
it is phenomena and appears as phenomena.
It is abiding, and it is liberation;
it is sitting, lying down, and walking.
It is life, and it is also cutting [life] off.[^T05-008]

<!-- pair: MTP-000205 -->

[Printed page 458.]

<!-- pair: MTP-000206 -->

The three worlds arise from that;
[it] appears as deities, mantra, and seals.
[It] arises as virtue and its result;
the ten perfections, too, are that.[^T05-009]

<!-- pair: MTP-000207 -->

The grounds and the qualities, too,
all arise from that itself.
Activity and cultivation itself,
view and all the rest also arise;
the vehicles in their greater and lesser stages, too,
all arise from that.[^T05-009]

<!-- pair: MTP-000208 -->

In brief, however they appear,
earth, water, fire, wind, and space—
the great elements—arise from that.[^T05-010]

<!-- pair: MTP-000209 -->

Gods, demigods, and humans,
hell beings, animals, and hungry ghosts[^T05-010]

<!-- pair: MTP-000210 -->

[Printed page 459.]

<!-- pair: MTP-000211 -->

arise authentically from that great [one].[^T05-010]

<!-- pair: MTP-000212 -->

Desire, hatred, bewilderment,
pride, jealousy, and the like—
magical display arises from that itself.[^T05-010]

<!-- pair: MTP-000213 -->

Ignorance, ordinary mind, and mental faculties,
aggregates, elements, and sense bases,
the collections of consciousness,
the five faculties and the like arise.[^T05-011]

<!-- pair: MTP-000214 -->

Sound, smell, taste,
objects of touch, and objects of desire
arise as its adornment.[^T05-012]

<!-- pair: MTP-000215 -->

Hollowness and insubstantiality,
lack of a core and the like, too,
arise as its expressiveness and play.[^T05-013]

<!-- pair: MTP-000216 -->

The all-basis and the appropriated
flesh, blood, lymph,
bones, skin, and body hair—
the assembled bodies arise from that.[^T05-014]

<!-- pair: MTP-000217 -->

Body, speech, and mental faculty,
mental factors and the various afflictions—
all arise from my own identity.[^T05-015]

<!-- pair: MTP-000218 -->

[Printed page 460.]

<!-- pair: MTP-000219 -->

Men, women, horses, and cattle—
all forms arise from that.
Eating, lying down, and sitting,
the various actions, arise.[^T05-015]

<!-- pair: MTP-000220 -->

O lord of wrathful ones, in the nature of phenomena abiding in this way,
there is no delusion from the beginning.
The dharma embodiment, like space,
is obscured by adventitious clouds of sentient beings.[^T05-001][^T05-016]

<!-- pair: MTP-000221 -->

Even the undeluded nature of phenomena
is endowed with an adventitious manner of delusion.
Momentary, together with causes and conditions,
although it appears thus with regard to objects,[^T05-016]

<!-- pair: MTP-000222 -->

in the primordial purity of the very beginning,
one cannot even say “delusion.”
Likewise, what could “undeluded” be?
Therefore, delusion is pure from the beginning.[^T05-017]

<!-- pair: MTP-000223 -->

In that great Ground-appearance itself,
because there is no designation as “ignorance,”[^T05-017]

<!-- pair: MTP-000224 -->

[Printed page 461.]

<!-- pair: MTP-000225 -->

there is no establishment as delusion.[^T05-017]

<!-- pair: MTP-000226 -->

Because “conceptual mind” is not imputed,
there is not even the name of stain or delusion.
Without the gathering of names and letters,
there is no imputed delusion.[^T05-017]

<!-- pair: MTP-000227 -->

Because, as the name “phenomena,” [retained: དུམ་གྲགས་པས],
there is not even the name of delusion imputed by the conceptual mind.
Because it is not established as ordinary mind and mental faculty,
the delusion that brings about movement is naturally absent.[^T05-018]

<!-- pair: MTP-000228 -->

Because there are no two stains, subtle and coarse,
delusion as a condition of arising is naturally absent.
Because doing and the doer are not two,
where is the delusion of apprehending objects?[^T05-019]

<!-- pair: MTP-000229 -->

Because objects and faculties are not two,
there is no delusion of apprehending objects.[^T05-019]

<!-- pair: MTP-000230 -->

O great lord of wrathful ones, listen well!
In the phenomena appearing as intrinsic nature,[^T05-001][^T05-019]
