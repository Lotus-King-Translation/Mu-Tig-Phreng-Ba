<!-- pair: MTP-000066 -->

[Electronic chapter marker 3.]

<!-- pair: MTP-000067 -->

Then Hayagrīva, king of the wrathful ones, petitioned in these words:[^T03-001][^T03-024]

<!-- pair: MTP-000068 -->

“O, O Bhagavān Vajradhara!
The authoritative teaching of self-awareness’s great primordial knowing—
please bestow [it], king of certainty!”[^T03-001][^T03-002][^T03-004][^T03-024]

<!-- pair: MTP-000069 -->

Then Bhagavān Vajradhara
arose from the seat where he did not abide.
Having uttered the word-formula [retained: སར་བ་འདུས་ཧ],
he spoke to the assembled gathering:[^T03-001][^T03-003]

<!-- pair: MTP-000070 -->

“O, O, listen, assembled retinue!
This blazing lamp that makes [things] appear,[^T03-003][^T03-024]

<!-- pair: MTP-000071 -->

[Printed page 430.]

<!-- pair: MTP-000072 -->

since [it is] inseparable from the dharma embodiment,
as for the way of activity of the object of self-awareness:
from the embodiment of means, discerning knowing itself—
everything plays in the unceasing expanse.[^T03-004][^T03-005][^T03-024]

<!-- pair: MTP-000073 -->

Since secret primordial knowing is unchanging,
the unceasing appearance of intrinsic nature
proliferates like the embodiment of essence itself.
Since [it is] far beyond both doing and the doer,
[it] sees and knows appearance in the object of the nature of phenomena.[^T03-006][^T03-024]

<!-- pair: MTP-000074 -->

The sphere of the lamp’s primordial knowing is clear.
In the embodiment of awareness, the nature of phenomena is clear.
[This] is the appearance of the lamp itself.
Then, since [it] opens the way for appearance,
the appearances of awareness are not different.[^T03-007][^T03-024]

<!-- pair: MTP-000075 -->

This vast universal seal of the secret,
since [it] joins equally with all buddhas—
in limitlessness, no center is an object of focus.[^T03-001][^T03-008][^T03-024]

<!-- pair: MTP-000076 -->

[Printed page 431.]

<!-- pair: MTP-000077 -->

Since ultimate primordial knowing appears as five,
conventional primordial knowing is varied:
[it] appears like embodiment, and awareness gathers.
Even varied appearances, subtle and coarse,
do not obscure the meaning of primordial knowing.[^T03-009]

<!-- pair: MTP-000078 -->

Like a butter lamp of a crown jewel,
this light of appearance is immeasurable.
Since the appearance of awareness is clear light,
the realms of the victors of the three embodiments are clear.[^T03-010][^T03-024]

<!-- pair: MTP-000079 -->

Since the precious pith instruction is manifest,
[one] reaches the far shore in the ground of awakening.
[It] is inexpressible, yet appears.[^T03-011]

<!-- pair: MTP-000080 -->

O, O, Lord of Secrets, holder of the vajra!
If one realizes the meaning of saṃsāra itself,
there is no such thing called “nirvāṇa.”
If one realizes the meaning of the five afflictions,[^T03-001][^T03-012]

<!-- pair: MTP-000081 -->

[Printed page 432.]

<!-- pair: MTP-000082 -->

primordial knowing does not exist as a name.
If one realizes the meaning of suffering itself,
there is no great bliss to seek.
If one thoroughly realizes the very limit of faults,
there is no need to seek great qualities.
If one realizes the meaning of delusion itself,
there is no need to seek awareness without delusion.[^T03-012]

<!-- pair: MTP-000083 -->

If one realizes the meaning of the five elements themselves,
the five great [ones] are accomplished naturally.
If the root of ignorance itself is ascertained,
the embodiment of awareness itself is accomplished without being accomplished.
If one knows the meaning of sentient beings themselves,
buddhas themselves are found without being sought.
If one realizes the meaning of varied appearances,
the light-appearance of self-awareness is found without being sought.[^T03-001][^T03-004][^T03-013][^T03-014][^T03-024]

<!-- pair: MTP-000084 -->

If one follows through conceptual thought itself,[^T03-014]

<!-- pair: MTP-000085 -->

[Printed page 433.]

<!-- pair: MTP-000086 -->

that very delusion is liberated in its own place.
If one realizes the meaning of empty appearance,
the embodiment of primordial knowing, too, is spontaneously present.
If one sees the depth of appearing as entities,
emptiness itself, too, is complete without being accomplished.
If one realizes the meaning of A, the nature of phenomena,
[one] reaches the far shore of the non-arising nature of phenomena.[^T03-011][^T03-014][^T03-015][^T03-024]

<!-- pair: MTP-000087 -->

Emaho!
The seal of the emptiness of self-appearance
is taught as stamped with the seal of knowing.[^T03-015][^T03-016][^T03-024]

<!-- pair: MTP-000088 -->

Boundless emptiness—the seal of the embodiment of primordial knowing.
Varied ordinary mind itself—the seal of awareness.
The perfectly pure, spacious expanse—the seal of primordial knowing.
Characteristics pure in actuality—the seal of self-awareness.
Appearance complete in itself—the seal of basic space.
Wondrous activity—the seal of appearance.
Wondrous cultivation—the seal of ordinary mind.[^T03-004][^T03-015][^T03-016][^T03-017][^T03-024]

<!-- pair: MTP-000089 -->

[Printed page 434.]

<!-- pair: MTP-000090 -->

Wondrous view—the seal of space.
Wondrous result—the seal of single resolution.
Wondrous primordial knowing—the seal of emptiness.
The wondrous nature of phenomena—the seal of play.
Wondrous realization—the seal of discerning knowing.
Wondrous essence—the seal of vajra chains.
Wondrous authenticity—the seal of awareness.[^T03-016][^T03-017][^T03-018][^T03-024]

<!-- pair: MTP-000091 -->

Emaho!
Listen, great Lord of Secrets.
The secret empowerment itself is utterly complete.
This instruction of the core’s vital essence—
in the core itself, the core is stable.[^T03-001][^T03-019]

<!-- pair: MTP-000092 -->

This pith instruction of “the three of certainty” [grouping unresolved]
becomes the ultimate chief of the tantras.
Since the ultimate and the conventional are nondual,[^T03-020][^T03-024]

<!-- pair: MTP-000093 -->

[Printed page 435.]

<!-- pair: MTP-000094 -->

there is great bliss in the object of self-appearance.
The sign that the nature of phenomena is nondual:
[it] does not follow after appearances,
and appears everywhere, yet is nondual.
Not abiding in the phenomena of doing and the doer,
[it] is nondual in the object of self-appearance.[^T03-020][^T03-021][^T03-024]

<!-- pair: MTP-000095 -->

Since [it] does not divide into one and many,
at the time of realization, [it] is nondual.
Since there is no hardship in the means of secret mantra,
at the time of understanding, [it] is nondual.[^T03-021][^T03-024]

<!-- pair: MTP-000096 -->

Although [it is] varied in the objects of delusion,
in the ultimate itself there is neither one nor two.
In outer and inner objects there is not even one;
at the time of knowing, variety arises.
Varied clinging is self-liberated.
Having understood the nature of phenomena, [one is] nondual, and[^T03-022][^T03-024]

<!-- pair: MTP-000097 -->

[Printed page 436.]

<!-- pair: MTP-000098 -->

if the kinds of delusory appearance are traced to their root,
the essence of self-awareness is nondual.
The perfectly pure realm is spontaneously present.”[^T03-004][^T03-014][^T03-015][^T03-022][^T03-024]

<!-- pair: MTP-000099 -->

Thus, from the precious secret tantra *The String of Pearls*: the third chapter, “Teaching How to Pass Beyond Delusion.”[^T03-001][^T03-023][^T03-024]
