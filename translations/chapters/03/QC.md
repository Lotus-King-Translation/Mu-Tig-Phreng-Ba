## Findings and dispositions

1. **CH03-Q01 — probable syntax/scope error; Medium severity, Moderate confidence; corrected with the alternative retained.** S334 `སྣ་ཚོགས་ཞེན་པ་རང་གྲོལ་ཏེ` initially read “Varied clinging is self-liberated.” The surrounding discussion of variety and the object-before-clinging construction favor “Clinging to variety is self-liberated.” T03-022 now identifies the object reading and preserves “varied clinging” as an alternative. U101 follows the revision. The established clinging and self-liberation assignments remain intact. This is a local attachment decision, not a claim that the alternative is impossible. Rules: Q3, Q5, Q7, Q9.

2. **CH03-Q02 — open/probable attachment issue; Medium potential severity, Moderate confidence; revised provisionally.** S294–S295 `རང་སྣང་སྟོང་པ་ཉིད་ཀྱི་རྒྱ / ཤེས་ཀྱི་རྒྱས་བཏབ་པ་བསྟན` initially became “The seal of the emptiness of self-appearance / is taught as stamped with the seal of knowing.” That reading supplies a genitive between self-appearance and emptiness. Following the adjacent nominal seal series, the revised wording is “Self-appearance—the seal of emptiness itself— / is taught as stamped with the seal of knowing.” T03-016 expressly retains the earlier genitive and an implicit-instruction reading as alternatives. The revision makes the structural choice visible without inventing a command sequence or expanding knowing into primordial knowing. Rules: Q2–Q3, Q7, Q9.

3. **CH03-Q03 — terminology/usage documentation gap; Low severity, High confidence in the gap; corrected.** S261 `མངོན་སྣང` initially became “is manifest” while U079 grouped it under ordinary canonical/grammatical appearance. “Manifest” is not an ordinary English inflection of appearance. The coordinator changed the line to “Since the precious pith instruction appears manifestly,” and T03-011, U105 and a separate eight-column proposal now disclose the local compound treatment. Appearance remains identifiable; the proposal does not activate a new canonical mapping. Rules: Part I §§3–4, 6, 8; Q5–Q7.

4. **CH03-Q04 — confirmed presentation defect; Low severity, High confidence; corrected.** The speech opens in pair 70 and continues through pair 98. The nested quoted name in pair 80 and numerical phrase in pair 92 now use single quotation marks inside that speech. No quoted meaning or source quantity changed. Rules: Q8–Q9.

5. **CH03-Q05 — source-note documentation gap; Low severity, High confidence; corrected.** C03-132 records a readable opening fragment `དེ་ཉིད` in an otherwise untranscribed smaller source note. The audit and assembled reader note now give the limited provisional sense “that very [thing]” or “that itself,” with referent and attachment unresolved. 6. **CH03-Q06 — author-proposed scope clarification and terminology refinement; reviewed and accepted locally, not newly approved terminology.** At S247 `རིག་པའི་སྣང་བ་ཐ་དད་མིན`, singular “the appearance of awareness is not different” avoids implying a comparison among multiple appearances.
