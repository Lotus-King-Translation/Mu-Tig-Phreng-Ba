# Golden v1 release policy

Governing source: `GOVERNING-WITNESS.json`. This task creates the maintained Tibetan edition, not an English translation.

Eight chapter releases are sequential. Chapter1 includes title/opening material; chapter8 includes the final root colophon. Stable electronic source anchors cover all2,053 payload lines, including blank and metadata lines. Electronic lineation is not physical print lineation. No original anchor is renumbered after a restoration.

Each active chapter freezes its electronic differences, structural anomalies, targeted scan checks and deliverables before editorial decisions are accepted. Every queued item receives an evidence-based disposition; uncertain readings can remain explicit. Untargeted anchors retain their exact base strings and are marked unreviewed. An unchanged electronic line is not certified scan agreement.

Source headings, source/page markers, root body, chapter colophons, annotations and seals remain distinct. The readable derivative may suppress page/control metadata from running Tibetan, while the machine reading and apparatus preserve every original anchor and its role. No punctuation normalization, language smoothing or conjectural Sanskrit reconstruction is permitted without a recorded governing-scan decision.

The two script renditions are one attributed transcript family. Transliteration conversion is a mechanical diagnostic, not an independent Tibetan witness. Adzom2000 can clarify the same printing base; it is not a second independent recension. Other collected manuscripts remain uncollated except any precisely recorded comparisons.

A chapter is publishable only after its frozen queues close, deterministic build/reconstruction/evidence checks pass, negative corruption tests reject, editorial review is recorded and final validation accepts a hash-bound signoff. The coordinator then publishes an annotated chapter tag and commits a receipt verifying its remote tag object and peeled commit. Previously released chapter artifacts remain byte-identical. An aggregate whole-work release follows all eight chapter releases.

Agent review is disclosed as such; no independent human palaeographic certification is claimed. Full scan proofreading and exhaustive witness collation remain false unless actually performed and separately documented. Source-derived Tibetan retains Wikisource attribution and CC BY-SA4.0; scan licensing remains source-specific.
