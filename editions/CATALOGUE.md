# String of Pearls — source catalogue

Discovery checked 2 October 2026. **This is a manifestation and research inventory, not a count of independent witnesses.** It consolidates the saved BDRC, rKTs, archival, bibliographic and electronic-text research. A catalogue-confirmed text is not necessarily an acquired or collated text. No governing witness, golden edition or translation has been selected or produced by this project.

The target is **མུ་ཏིག་རིན་པོ་ཆེ་ཕྲེང་བའི་རྒྱུད་**, *mu tig rin po che phreng ba'i rgyud*, also *mu tig phreng ba*, String of Pearls / Pearl Necklace / Necklace of Precious Pearls. Identity anchors: [rKTs G60](https://www.rkts.org/cat.php?id=60&typ=3), [BDRC WA0RG0060](https://library.bdrc.io/show/bdr:WA0RG0060), parallel BDRC work clusters [WA0XL6A6ADDB434FB](https://library.bdrc.io/show/bdr:WA0XL6A6ADDB434FB) and [WA0XL60123BEBEC01](https://library.bdrc.io/show/bdr:WA0XL60123BEBEC01).

Use [REGISTER.csv](REGISTER.csv) for acquired-item identities and hashes, [BOUNDARIES.md](BOUNDARIES.md) for visual start/end checks, and the source-specific metadata for retrieval/licensing. `ff.` means folios; `pp.` means printed pages; `images` means catalogue/manifest indices. These coordinate systems must not be substituted for one another. Whole shared boundary pages may contain adjacent works.
