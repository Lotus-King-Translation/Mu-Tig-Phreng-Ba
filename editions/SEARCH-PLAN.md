# Source discovery and acquisition plan — 2026-10-02

## Identity and aliases

Root tantra: མུ་ཏིག་ཕྲེང་བ་, mu tig phreng ba; also search mu tig phreng ba'i rgyud, rin po che mu tig phreng ba, Pearl Necklace, String of Pearls, Garland of Pearls, and French Collier/Guirlande de Perles. Verify full titles and opening/closing boundaries before identifying an item as the root tantra.

## Three discovery streams

1. BDRC work/instance/part graph; Seventeen Tantras printings/manuscripts and Nyingma Gyübum collections; linked IIIF and Internet Archive assets.
2. rKTs and scholarly catalogues, British Library EAP, Nepal/Bhutan archival leads, Spiti and Taipei reprints, international library holdings.
3. Tibetan electronic texts (Wikisource, Tsadra, Adarsha, Esukhia, OpenPecha, Webuddhist), translations and commentaries, with language and physical source recorded where known.

## Deliverables

- Exact title identity, aliases and exclusions.
- editions/REGISTER.csv with stable source IDs, physical basis, independence status, source URLs, stored paths and SHA-256.
- Human-readable edition catalogue and acquisition statuses.
- Saved catalogue metadata and reproducible retrieval details.
- Accessible source scans/texts where obtainable, with extent and restrictions recorded.
- Search log, negative results, unresolved leads and next actions.

Each stream records its searched endpoints and coverage. Discovery completion means this search pass is documented; it does not assert that no unrecorded witness exists. No paywall, login or provider access restriction is to be bypassed. A catalogue entry is not an acquired scan; different manifestations are not automatically independent witnesses.

## Validation

Verify local hashes, nonempty assets, parseable metadata, unique source IDs and existing registered paths. Inspect title/opening and closing images when extracting root-only facsimiles; distinguish catalogue-bounded acquisition from visually verified boundaries. No scan proofreading or textual collation is claimed at this phase.
